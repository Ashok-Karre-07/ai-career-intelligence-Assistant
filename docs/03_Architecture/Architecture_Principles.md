# Purpose

This document defines the architectural principles that govern the design, implementation, deployment, and evolution of the AI Career Intelligence Platform.

These principles serve as the foundation for all architecture decisions and ensure consistency throughout the project.

---

# Vision

Build an Enterprise-grade AI SaaS Platform that is:

* AI First
* Cloud Native
* Modular
* Secure
* Scalable
* Observable
* Extensible
* Maintainable

---

# Architectural Goals

The platform shall:

* Support thousands of concurrent users.
* Be deployable on any cloud provider.
* Support future migration to microservices.
* Provide reusable AI capabilities.
* Enable rapid feature delivery.
* Maintain high security standards.
* Minimize operational complexity.
* Support enterprise customers.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. AI-First Design

Artificial Intelligence is the core capability of the platform.

Every feature should evaluate whether AI improves the user experience before implementing traditional workflows.

Examples:

* Resume Analysis
* Job Matching
* Company Intelligence
* Interview Coaching
