# Purpose

This document defines the long-term product roadmap for the AI Career Intelligence Platform. It describes the planned evolution of the platform from the initial MVP to an enterprise-grade AI-powered career ecosystem.

The roadmap provides strategic direction for engineering, product management, architecture, and future business growth.

# Version 1.0 – MVP

## Goal

Validate the product with early users and deliver a complete end-to-end international job search experience.

### Major Capabilities

- User Authentication
- User Profile
- Resume Upload
- Resume Parsing
- Resume Management
- AI Resume Tailoring
- AI Job Search
- AI Job Matching
- Company Intelligence
- Application Tracker
- Dashboard

### Success Metrics

- 1,000 Registered Users
- 100 Daily Active Users
- 80% Job Match Accuracy
- 90% Resume Parsing Accuracy
- AI Response Time < 5 Seconds

# Version 2.0 – AI Career Coach

## Goal

### New Features

- Learning Recommendations
- Certification Recommendations
- Career Roadmaps

- AI Cover Letter Generator

### Business Benefits

- Higher user engagement
- Improved interview success
- Increased user retention
