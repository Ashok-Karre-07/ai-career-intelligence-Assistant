# Purpose

This document describes the business problems that the AI Career Intelligence Platform aims to solve. It identifies the current challenges faced by international job seekers and explains why an AI-driven platform is needed.

---

# Background

The global job market has become increasingly competitive. Professionals seeking employment in international markets often interact with multiple systems throughout their job search journey.

Typical activities include:

- Searching jobs on multiple job portals
- Customizing resumes for every application
- Researching companies
- Understanding visa sponsorship availability
- Preparing for interviews
- Tracking applications
- Communicating with recruiters

Each activity requires different platforms, resulting in a fragmented and inefficient experience.

---

# Existing Challenges

## 1. Fragmented Job Search

Candidates search across LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, company career portals, recruitment agencies, and many other websites.

Problems:

- Duplicate searches
- Duplicate applications
- Missed opportunities
- Time-consuming process

---

## 2. Resume Customization

Every company expects resumes tailored to its Job Description.

Current process:

- Manual editing
- ATS keyword research
- Multiple resume versions
- High effort

---

## 3. Company Research

Candidates spend significant time gathering information such as:

- Company overview
- Technologies used
- Work culture
- Salary ranges
- Visa sponsorship
- Hiring trends

Information is scattered across different websites.

---

## 4. Lack of Skill Gap Visibility

Most candidates do not know:

- Which skills are missing
- Which certifications to pursue
- Which projects improve employability

Learning paths are often unclear.

---

## 5. Application Tracking

Candidates frequently apply to hundreds of jobs.

Current tracking methods include:

- Excel sheets
- Notes
- Emails
- Browser bookmarks

These methods become difficult to maintain.

---

## 6. Interview Preparation

Preparing for interviews requires multiple resources:

- Coding platforms
- AI tools
- Company interview experiences
- Behavioral question banks

No unified platform exists.

---

## 7. Visa Information

International candidates struggle to identify:

- Companies offering visa sponsorship
- Country-specific work visa requirements
- Immigration pathways

Reliable information is difficult to consolidate.

---

# Business Problem

Current career platforms solve isolated problems.

For example:

- LinkedIn → Networking
- Glassdoor → Company Reviews
- Indeed → Job Listings
- ChatGPT → General AI Assistance

No platform provides a complete AI-powered international career ecosystem.

---

# Opportunity

Artificial Intelligence can unify these disconnected workflows into a single intelligent platform capable of:

- Understanding user profiles
- Recommending suitable jobs
- Tailoring resumes
- Identifying skill gaps
- Researching companies
- Tracking applications
- Preparing interviews

---

# Proposed Solution

The AI Career Intelligence Platform will provide an end-to-end AI-powered career management system.

Core capabilities include:

- AI Resume Intelligence
- AI Job Discovery
- AI Job Matching
- Company Intelligence
- Recruiter Intelligence
- Visa Intelligence
- Interview Preparation
- Career Coaching
- Application Tracking

---

# Expected Benefits

## For Candidates

- Save time during job search
- Improve resume quality
- Increase interview opportunities
- Track applications efficiently
- Receive personalized recommendations

---

## For Recruiters

- Better candidate profiles
- Faster resume evaluation
- Improved hiring decisions

---

## For Organizations

- Improved talent discovery
- Better candidate engagement
- AI-assisted recruitment insights

---

# Success Criteria

The identified business problems are considered solved when users can:

- Discover relevant international jobs from one platform
- Tailor resumes within minutes
- Receive AI-powered career recommendations
- Track all applications centrally
- Access company and visa insights from a unified dashboard

This document defines the current problems and business opportunities that justify the development of the AI Career Intelligence Platform.
