# Purpose

This document defines the strategic business goals of the AI Career Intelligence Platform. These goals align the product vision with measurable business outcomes and guide product planning, development, and future growth.

---

# Business Objectives

The primary objective of the AI Career Intelligence Platform is to become the preferred AI-powered platform for professionals seeking international career opportunities.

The platform will enable users to discover jobs, optimize resumes, prepare for interviews, research companies, and manage applications through one intelligent ecosystem.

---

# Strategic Goals

## Goal 1 – Simplify International Job Search

### Objective

Reduce the complexity of finding international job opportunities.

### Success Indicators

- Aggregate jobs from multiple sources.
- Enable AI-powered job search.
- Personalized job recommendations.
- Country and visa-based filtering.

---

## Goal 2 – Improve Resume Quality

### Objective

Help users create ATS-friendly resumes tailored to each job description.

### Success Indicators

- AI Resume Tailoring
- ATS Compatibility Score
- Resume Version Management
- Keyword Optimization

---

## Goal 3 – Increase Interview Success

### Objective

Improve interview conversion rates through AI-powered preparation.

### Success Indicators

- Mock Interview Sessions
- Technical Question Generation
- Behavioral Interview Guidance
- Company-specific Interview Preparation

---

## Goal 4 – Provide Company Intelligence

### Objective

Allow candidates to make informed career decisions.

### Success Indicators

- Company Overview
- Tech Stack
- Hiring Trends
- Salary Insights
- Visa Sponsorship Information

---

## Goal 5 – Deliver Personalized Career Guidance

### Objective

Use Artificial Intelligence to recommend personalized career improvements.

### Success Indicators

- Skill Gap Analysis
- Career Roadmaps
- Certification Recommendations
- Learning Suggestions

---

## Goal 6 – Centralize Job Application Management

### Objective

Provide a single dashboard to manage all job applications.

### Success Indicators

- Application Tracking
- Interview Tracking
- Recruiter Communication History
- Offer Tracking
- Rejection Analysis

---

# Business Risks

Potential risks include:

- Limited access to external job data.
- Changes in recruitment APIs.
- AI model inaccuracies.
- High infrastructure costs.
- Data privacy regulations.
- Rapid changes in immigration policies.

---

# Assumptions

This project assumes:

- Users are willing to use AI recommendations.
- Resume parsing quality remains high.
- Job data can be collected from supported sources.
- Cloud infrastructure can scale as adoption grows.
