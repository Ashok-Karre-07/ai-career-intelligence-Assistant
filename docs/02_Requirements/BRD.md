# Purpose

The purpose of this Business Requirements Document (BRD) is to define the business objectives, scope, stakeholders, business requirements, and success criteria for the AI Career Intelligence Platform.

This document serves as the primary reference for product planning and provides a common understanding of the business goals before technical implementation begins.

---

# Business Background

International job seekers currently rely on multiple disconnected platforms to manage their career journey.

Typical workflow includes:

- Searching jobs
- Building resumes
- Tailoring resumes
- Company research
- Interview preparation
- Application tracking
- Recruiter communication

The absence of a unified platform increases complexity and reduces efficiency.

The AI Career Intelligence Platform aims to centralize these activities into a single AI-powered ecosystem.
